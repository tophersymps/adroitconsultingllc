Interaction notes

for steel

Ignition sequence: lit stars surge on one-by-one in lesson order, ~120ms stagger, scale + bloom ramp. The signature moment.

Raycast hover: star lifts (scale + z-offset) + lesson tooltip. ~150ms.

Click-to-fly: camera tweens to the star (ease-in-out, ~600ms) → navigates to the lesson.

Bloom: UnrealBloom, strength 0.7, threshold 0.85, radius 0.5. Only lit/current/complete stars bloom.
