Constellations + Chronicle

Chart a sky. Ignite a star.

Star-ignition achievement layer on the Fortress-of-Solitude sky. Every course is a constellation — one star per lesson, lit as you complete it. Your record is the Chronicle; your mastery climbs the rank ladder from starseed to celestial.

05 Responsive behavior

Desktop ≥1024

  • Series constellation = connected path (stars + rails)
  • Full sky = Chronicle + rank ladder side-by-side
  • Hub preview = compact star row in PathCard gradient band (stays light)

Mobile ≤375px

  • Connectors drop → starfield grid (wrapped dots, 4–5/row)
  • Stat strip stacks to 1 column
  • Chronicle becomes full-width list
  • Interactive stars: hit target ≥44px
