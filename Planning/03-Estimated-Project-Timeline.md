# Estimated Project Timeline

---

## Duration

- **Standard scope:** 10–12 weeks
- **Accelerated option:** 8–9 weeks (requires locked scope and expedited client approvals)

---

## Phase Plan

### Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy — Weeks 1–2

- Stakeholder interviews and business context gathering
- Messaging alignment and competitive positioning review
- Information architecture definition and approval
- Technical spike for integration requirements (CRM, analytics, forms)

**Exit criteria:** Approved IA document, confirmed messaging direction, integration requirements documented.

---

### Phase 2: UX Direction & Content Production — Weeks 3–4

- Wireframes for all core pages (desktop + mobile)
- Content outlines and starter copy finalization
- Brand treatment and visual direction approval
- Component library definition

**Exit criteria:** Signed-off wireframes, approved copy for all 5 pages, confirmed visual direction.

---

### Phase 3: Technical Build — Weeks 5–8

- Front-end implementation (responsive, accessible, performant)
- CMS or platform integration and content population
- Contact form workflows, lead routing, and CRM integration
- SEO instrumentation, analytics tagging, and consent setup
- Internal dev QA and defect resolution

**Exit criteria:** Feature-complete site in staging environment, all content populated, integrations functional.

---

### Phase 4: QA, UAT & Launch Prep — Weeks 9–10

- Cross-browser and cross-device validation
- Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.1 AA target)
- Performance audit (Core Web Vitals targets)
- Client UAT with structured feedback capture
- Issue resolution and launch readiness checklist

**Exit criteria:** UAT sign-off, all critical/high issues resolved, launch checklist complete.

---

### Phase 5: Launch & Stabilization — Weeks 11–12 (optional)

- DNS cutover and production deployment
- Post-launch monitoring (uptime, performance, error tracking)
- Rapid-response issue resolution window
- Knowledge transfer and admin documentation handoff

**Exit criteria:** Site live, monitoring confirmed, handoff documentation delivered.

---

## Milestone Summary

| ID | Milestone | Target |
|----|-----------|--------|
| M1 | Discovery Sign-Off | End of Week 2 |
| M2 | UX/Content Sign-Off | End of Week 4 |
| M3 | Build Complete | End of Week 8 |
| M4 | UAT Sign-Off | End of Week 10 |
| M5 | Launch Complete | Week 10–12 |

---

## Dependencies & Assumptions

- Client feedback turnaround: 2–3 business days per review cycle
- One primary stakeholder group handles approvals
- Brand assets (logo, photography, color palette) available at project start
- Legal/privacy copy provided or approved by client counsel
- Hosting environment provisioned by end of Phase 1
