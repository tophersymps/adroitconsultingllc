# Pitch Presentation Outline

> Designed for a stakeholder-facing PowerPoint deck to secure approval and budget for the Adroit Consulting premium website initiative.

---

## Slide 1 — Title Slide

- **Title:** Adroit Consulting — Premium Website Initiative
- **Subtitle:** Enterprise Positioning, Lead Quality, and Delivery Credibility
- **Footer:** Presenter name | Date | Confidential

---

## Slide 2 — Executive Summary

- **Why now:** Current digital presence under-represents the caliber of consulting Adroit delivers.
- **What we are building:** A premium enterprise consulting website aligned to how enterprise buyers evaluate and select partners.
- **What success looks like:** Stronger trust signals, higher-quality inbound inquiries, and a scalable platform for growth.

---

## Slide 3 — The Opportunity

- Fragmented or outdated digital messaging erodes confidence during the enterprise evaluation process.
- Service value and delivery track record are significantly higher than current web expression conveys.
- A modern site becomes a competitive differentiator and a prerequisite for enterprise procurement shortlists.
- Scalable platform needed to support future thought leadership, case studies, and partner content.

---

## Slide 4 — Target Audience & Buying Committee

- **Primary decision-makers:** COO, CIO, VP Operations, Salesforce Product Owners
- **Supporting influencers:** IT architecture leads, procurement, marketing operations
- **What they evaluate:** Risk reduction, execution certainty, measurable outcomes, and cultural fit
- **Implication:** Site must communicate credibility, process maturity, and business acumen — not just technical capability.

---

## Slide 5 — Strategic Positioning

- **Brand promise:** Precision consulting with measurable business impact.
- **Three pillars:** Salesforce Consulting | Business Process Automation | Web Development
- **Tone:** Premium, authoritative, outcomes-driven
- **Visual direction:** Logo-forward identity, FC Barcelona-inspired palette (carmine + navy), enterprise neutrals

---

## Slide 6 — Proposed Information Architecture

- **Core pages:** Home, Salesforce Consulting, Business Process Automation, Web Development, Contact
- **Conversion path:** Credibility (Home) → Service detail → Consultation request (Contact)
- **Scalable structure:** Ready for future additions — case studies, insights/blog, careers, partner portal
- **Navigation:** Clean top-level nav; no dropdowns for initial launch; clear CTAs on every page

---

## Slide 7 — Content & Messaging Framework

- Executive-first copywriting style: lead with outcomes, support with capabilities
- Each service page follows a consistent structure: hero, positioning, modules, outcomes, CTA
- CTA strategy drives toward discovery sessions and assessments — not generic "contact us"
- Content is ready for SEO instrumentation and social sharing metadata

---

## Slide 8 — Visual Direction

- Unchanged Adroit Consulting logo mark as central brand anchor
- FC Barcelona-inspired accent palette:
  - Deep Carmine `#A50044`
  - Navy Blue `#004D98`
- Enterprise neutrals: Charcoal `#1F2937`, Light Gray `#F5F7FA`
- High-contrast, premium layouts with clear typographic hierarchy
- Photography direction: professional environments, technology, enterprise collaboration

---

## Slide 9 — Technology Options

- Three viable stacks evaluated: **React (Next.js)**, **WordPress**, **Salesforce Experience Cloud**
- Trade-off lens: creative control, speed to launch, governance, scalability, TCO
- Detailed comparison in supporting document (`05-Tech-Stack-Recommendation.md`)
- Recommended path presented with rationale and migration considerations

---

## Slide 10 — Delivery Method

- **Phase 1:** Discovery & Strategy (stakeholder interviews, IA, messaging alignment)
- **Phase 2:** UX Direction & Content Production (wireframes, copy, brand treatment)
- **Phase 3:** Technical Build (platform implementation, integrations, QA foundation)
- **Phase 4:** QA, UAT & Launch Prep (cross-device testing, accessibility, launch checklist)
- **Phase 5:** Launch & Stabilization (go-live, monitoring, handoff)
- Decision gates and stakeholder reviews built into each phase transition

---

## Slide 11 — Timeline & Milestones

- **Total duration:** 10–12 weeks (standard scope)
- **Accelerated option:** 8–9 weeks with locked scope and expedited approvals

| Milestone | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Discovery Sign-Off | End of Week 2 |
| UX/Content Sign-Off | End of Week 4 |
| Build Complete | End of Week 8 |
| UAT Sign-Off | End of Week 10 |
| Launch | Week 10–12 |

---

## Slide 12 — Commercial Scope (SOW Summary)

- **In scope:** Strategy, design, content, platform build, CRM integration, QA, launch support
- **Deliverables:** Production website, admin guidance, technical handoff docs, post-launch support
- **Assumptions:** Timely client feedback, single approval group, brand assets available
- **Out of scope:** Multi-language, advanced personalization, legacy content migration, ongoing managed marketing
- Full SOW detail in supporting document (`04-SOW-Summary.md`)

---

## Slide 13 — Risk Management

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Content review delays | Defined feedback SLAs per phase |
| Integration complexity | Early technical spike in discovery |
| Scope creep | Change order process with impact assessment |
| Review bottlenecks | Single stakeholder approval authority |
| Quality gaps | Automated performance/accessibility testing |

---

## Slide 14 — Expected Outcomes

- Improved enterprise perception and shortlist positioning
- Increased volume and quality of inbound consulting inquiries
- Better alignment between marketing presence, sales conversations, and delivery brand
- Scalable foundation for future content, portals, and partner experiences
