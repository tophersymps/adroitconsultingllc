Summary

The blueprint for Chris's four Learn items already existed as 19 cards. This pass reconciles those 19 against the four items and Chris's constraints, confirms that no card builds lesson gating and no card adds a second completion path, adds the desktop-and-mobile acceptance criteria the amendment requires, and groups the work into four waves that open one after the other.

Outcome: all 19 cards are retained. Five cards carry an amendment. The work ships in four waves: exam naming first, then design and foundations, then the learner-visible surfaces, then integration and evidence. A wave opens only when the previous wave is merged and deployed, not when it is reviewed.

Scope boundaries

In scope: lesson page composition, series syllabus check nodes, exam naming across every listed surface, the tests that lock both labels, and desktop plus mobile evidence for each card.

Out of scope: rewriting lesson or exam content beyond the 12 exam titles, any change to pass marks, any lesson-level authorization model, blog citation placement and naming, design rework beyond a note, and emails or marketing copy outside the app.

Accepted limitations, documented so they are not filed as bugs: lessons with no narration have no audio bar, so the hero control stays the only way to mark them; guests keep the existing locked player card and the hero control; the paywall peek keeps citations inside the article; a check never hides a lesson and a lesson never hides a check.

Handoff

Filing: Wave 1 only (B1 to B6). Waves 2 to 4 are filed the day the previous wave merges. Pre-filing them would invite parallel starts, which is the thing Chris asked to avoid.

To the implementer: the API contracts are the seam between parallel cards. If a signature has to change, change the contract in the same pull request. Three rules: no new write path for completion, no lesson gating of any kind, and do not decide the Agentic AI kind or the pass mark.

To the designer: K1 decides whether item 2 works. The node has to be visible in the timeline at zero progress, not only once the learner reaches its range.

To QA: each card needs desktop and mobile evidence. A card with one viewport class is unfinished. The accepted limitations above are not defects.
