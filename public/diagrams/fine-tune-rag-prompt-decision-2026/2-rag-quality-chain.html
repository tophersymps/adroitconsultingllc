The trap A wrong chunk steers the model toward a confident wrong answer. Most "RAG does not work" stories are retrieval problems, not generation ones, so fine-tuning fixes nothing here.

Order to tune first: chunking, then reranking, then embeddings. Each is cheaper to fix than any training run.
