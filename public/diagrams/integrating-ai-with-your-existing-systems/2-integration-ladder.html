The integration ladder: five rungs, five decisions

1

Manual

A person copies text in and the answer out. The human is the integration and the control. Acceptable for low sensitivity.

2

Retrieval over managed content

The AI reads a curated corpus: policies, rate sheets, wikis. Decide which corpus, who curates it, what is excluded. Most visible value lives here.

3

Read access to live systems of record

CRM, ticketing, orders. Two new questions appear: whose permissions does the connection use, and is the fetched data the right version?

4

Write access

The AI creates, updates, or sends. A wrong output is now a business event. Decisions shift to consequence: maximum damage, approval, daily cap, reversal.

5

Embedded integration

The AI lives inside software where work already happens, on the vendor's terms. Usually arrives as an upgrade, not a purchase.

One rule for the wall: a use case may climb one rung per quarter, and it must demonstrate value at the rung it currently occupies before it climbs.
