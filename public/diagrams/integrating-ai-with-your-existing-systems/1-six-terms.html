The six integration terms that make the conversation survivable

Integration Surface

Every place where AI can see your data or change your systems. The chat box, a connector, an embedded feature nobody told you about.

Connector

A pre-built path to read or act on another system. Underneath, every connector is an API call with a credential attached.

API

The programmatic door into a system, distinct from the screen. The owner's question is never whether one exists, it is who holds the key.

Retrieval (RAG)

The AI fetches relevant passages at question time instead of having learned your data. The index can be stale, and it can contain things the asker should not see.

Read vs Write Scope

Fetch only, or fetch and change. Nearly every frightening enterprise story is a write-scope story in a read-scope costume.

Source of Truth

The one system whose version of a fact wins when two disagree. Integration disputes are usually unsettled source-of-truth disputes.
