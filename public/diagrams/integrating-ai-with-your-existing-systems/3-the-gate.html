The gate: five questions before any connection is built

1

What is the source of truth, and is that the system we are connecting?

If the answer is "it depends which field," the connection is not ready. You are about to build a fast path to a disagreement.

2

Read or write?

If write, what is the worst realistic outcome of one wrong write, and who approves before it lands? Rank by reversibility, not likelihood.

3

Whose permissions does the connection use?

Mirroring means the AI reaches only what the asking user could already open. A shared service credential is easier and usually over-permissioned.

4

What happens when this breaks, and how would we know within a day?

Every connection breaks. Ask for a named breakage signal, not a promise of uptime.

5

Who owns this row in twelve months?

A person, not a team, and the row carries a review date. An unowned connection is integration debt with a dashboard.

Add a sixth question for anything that can act: what could this be tricked into doing by the content it reads? The mitigation is least privilege, not a better prompt.
