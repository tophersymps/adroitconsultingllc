Thea's first-year shape: one connection buys, one is refused

Freight brokerage, 1,240 employees, 60 account managers. Leadership wants "ask our systems anything."

MEASURE

Time log first

Nine account managers log two weeks of lookups. 38 minutes a day lost, which is 38 working hours a day across 60 people.

Rate sheets and customer terms: approved and funded

Shipment status: rebuilt on mirrored permissions

The restricted-user test read an account that user cannot open, because the connector used a shared service credential. Rebuilt on mirrored permissions for four more days. Acceptance test is now permanent: three correct answers and one refusal.

A wrong rate quote is a commercial commitment. Runs with eight users, approval before send, and a fifty-record daily cap for one quarter. Lifts only if approval stays above 95 percent and error stays under 2 percent.
