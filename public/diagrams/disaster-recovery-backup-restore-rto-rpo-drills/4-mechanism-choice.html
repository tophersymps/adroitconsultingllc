Pick the backup mechanism from the RPO

The more data you cannot afford to lose, the faster the recovery mechanism has to run

How much data loss is acceptable (RPO)?
Near zero · seconds to minutes
Replication layer

Financial and customer records: asynchronous event streaming or Change Data Capture keeps a near current copy ready to recover. A daily backup alone cannot hit this.

Hours
Scheduled sync + daily backup

Analytics and internal operational data: a daily backup service plus a scheduled sync holds data loss to hours and keeps a point in time restore available.

What does the backlog of protection look like?
Days accepted
Data Export Service

Historical reporting and archival data: weekly exports in enterprise editions, monthly otherwise. Coarse and cheap, fits a coarse RPO. Patch with a plan for the Recycle Bin.

Whole org, point in time
Backup and Recover service

Independent daily backups with compare and restore of selected records and metadata. Brings an aggressive RPO and a real restore path instead of a CSV reload.

Rule of thumb: never back an aggressive target with a coarse mechanism. Pair the RPO with a mechanism that can actually deliver it, then prove it with a drill.
