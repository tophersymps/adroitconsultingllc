Disaster recovery is a loop, not an artifact

Measurable targets set the strategy, and a drill proves it, feeding lessons back

1 · TARGETS
RTO and RPO per capability

Business agrees how fast recovery must happen and how much data loss is acceptable, set per capability, not org wide.

2 · MECHANISM
Backup and restore strategy

Pick the tooling that actually hits the target: exports for coarse RPO, a backup service and Change Data Capture for tighter ones.

3 · DRILL
Executed restore test

Restore into a sandbox under timed conditions. Measure achieved RTO and actual data loss against the targets.

4 · LEARN
Lessons into the runbook

Document what the drill revealed, fix replication and procedure gaps, update the runbook, repeat next quarter.

⟶ targets drive mechanism ⟶ drill validates mechanism ⟶ lessons tighten targets ⟶

A backup you have never restored is not a backup. The loop only means something when step three is real and scheduled.
