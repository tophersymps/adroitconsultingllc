RTO and RPO are set per capability

Critical data gets tight targets and expensive mechanisms; archival data gets coarse ones and a cheap export

CapabilityRTO (time to recover)RPO (acceptable data loss)Backing mechanism
Revenue-critical operationsMinutes to 1 hourNear zero (seconds)Asynchronous event streaming, hot standby
Customer-facing services1 to 4 hoursNear zero (minutes)Change Data Capture, backup service
Internal tools4 to 24 hoursHoursScheduled sync, daily backup service
Historical reportingDaysDaysWeekly or monthly data export

The trap: a uniform "everything recovers in an hour" target over-invests in non-critical data and under-invests in critical data.

Set each row with business stakeholder approval, then back every target with a mechanism that can actually hit it.
