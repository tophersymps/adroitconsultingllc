The target ladder: 99% standard; 99.5% redundancy + monitoring; 99.9% multi-region failover; 99.95% active-active + chaos; 99.99% multi-org.

Trap: when the platform SLA and your solution SLO are both 99.9%, any significant platform downtime consumes your full budget, leaving no buffer for app-layer failures, integration issues, or planned maintenance in the same window.

Set the solution SLO below the platform SLA, promise only what the architecture can hit, and measure per business capability, not per technical component.
