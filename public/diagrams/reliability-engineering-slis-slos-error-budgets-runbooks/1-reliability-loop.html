The reliability-engineering loop

Measurement, target, budget, and response feed each other so "reliable enough" stays a number the team can defend or spend

SLI: measure

Service level indicator. Share of good events over total events, from the user's point of view. Availability, latency, success rate.

↓

SLO: agree

Service level objective. A target value for the SLI, set per business capability with stakeholders, over a defined window, below the platform SLA.

↓

Error budget: spend

100% minus the SLO. A 99.9% SLO leaves a 0.1% budget. Within budget, releases proceed. Budget blown over four weeks, non-critical changes halt.

↓

Runbook: respond

When an alert fires, the runbook maps it to a specific first action for a calm stranger. Postmortems feed lessons back into the SLO targets.

Each stage is useless alone. Measurement without a target is a graph. A target without a budget is a wish. A budget without a runbook leaves the on-call person guessing.
